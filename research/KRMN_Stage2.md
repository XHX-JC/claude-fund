## BUSINESS — WHAT DOES KRMN DO

Karman designs and manufactures critical hardware for US government space and defense
programmes. Products: payload protection and deployment systems (fairings, nosecones),
aerodynamic interstage systems, propulsion system components, munitions, hypersonic
structures, UAS launchers, and maritime defense systems. End customers: US DoD
(missile/munitions), NASA, SpaceX, ULA, commercial launch providers.

Founded 2020, IPO February 2025 at $22/share. PE-backed: Trive Capital (TCFIII).
Headquarters: Huntington Beach, CA. 28% CAGR in revenue 2022-2025.

Key differentiator: Karman manufactures components that cannot easily be substituted
midstream on an active DoD programme. Once qualified on a missile programme (e.g.
GMLRS, Tomahawk, hypersonics), it is effectively sole-sourced for the programme life.
The customer switching cost is years of re-qualification.

## GREENSHOE DYNAMICS — CRITICAL FOR ENTRY TIMING

Secondary priced May 28 at $61. Underwriters were granted a 30-day greenshoe option
(right to buy 2.025M additional shares from the PE sponsor).

Standard greenshoe mechanism:
  - Underwriters oversell by 2.025M shares at $61 (short position)
  - If price falls below $61: underwriters buy stock in open market to cover short
    (stabilisation buying — price support below $61)
  - If price rises above $61: they exercise the option instead

Since KRMN has been trading at $49-60 since the secondary:
  - Underwriters are actively buying in the market (stabilisation)
  - This buying expires June 27 (30 days from May 28)
