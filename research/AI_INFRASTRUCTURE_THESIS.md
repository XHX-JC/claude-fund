## THEMATIC OBSERVATIONS — WHY THIS IS NOT LIKE PREVIOUS CYCLES

1. **Hyperscaler capex is contracted, not discretionary.** CRWV backlog $66.8B, Credo customer LTAs, Modine 5-year visibility, MU HBM sold out. This is fundamentally different from the 2000 dot-com buildout which was speculative.

2. **Physical bottlenecks are real and persistent.** Transformer lead times of 3-5 years cannot be fixed with software. Grid capacity limits where data centers can physically be built. This supports pricing power in equipment suppliers.

3. **EDA tools are a toll on ALL chip design.** Every custom ASIC, every GPU, every TPU routes through SNPS/CDNS. This is the cleanest picks-and-shovels play in the thesis.

4. **Memory is moving from commodity to contracted.** MU selling HBM via multi-year LTAs at specific price/volume is structurally different from DRAM spot market dynamics. This may not cycle the way prior memory upturns did.

5. **Power is the binding constraint through 2030.** Every thesis — cooling, networking, compute — is gated by available MW. Companies that own grid-adjacent capacity (CEG, TLN, VST held) benefit structurally.
