## BUSINESS — WHAT ASTS DOES

AST SpaceMobile is building a global broadband network from space that works with
ordinary smartphones — no satellite phone, no additional hardware. The satellite
array is large enough to communicate directly with standard LTE/5G handsets using
existing terrestrial spectrum held by partner carriers.

Key IP: phased-array antenna technology. BlueBird 6 — the largest commercial
communications array ever deployed in LEO — demonstrated 98.9 Mbps peak speeds.
Block 2 (BB8+) satellites will greatly exceed this.

Revenue model: ASTS charges MNO partners (AT&T, Verizon, etc.) for access to the
SpaceMobile network. Partners pass on a connectivity fee to consumers. ASTS collects
a per-subscriber or usage-based fee. The long-term economics are excellent IF the
constellation reaches scale — near-zero marginal cost per additional user once
satellites are in orbit.

Current partners: AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, Rakuten, Google, Bell, Telus, stc Group,
American Tower. ~60 MNO partners globally covering 3+ billion subscribers.
stc Group (Saudi telecom) prepaid $175M. $1.2B in total contracted commitments.

FCC status: Supplemental Coverage from Space authorisation granted — up to 248
satellites authorised for commercial service in the US.
