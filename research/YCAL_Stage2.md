## BUSINESS — WHAT IS YCA

Yellow Cake plc is a Jersey-domiciled company that buys and holds physical uranium
oxide concentrate (U3O8 — "yellowcake") for long-term capital appreciation.

It is NOT a miner. It has no production risk, no operating cost inflation, no
permitting risk. It is a pure exposure vehicle to the uranium spot price.

Unique advantage: Offtake agreement with Kazatomprom (JSC National Atomic Company,
Kazakhstan — the world's largest uranium producer at ~40% of global supply). This
agreement gives Yellow Cake the right to purchase up to $100M/year of U3O8 at spot
price directly from Kazatomprom, providing priority supply access unavailable to
most investors and institutions.

Holdings at date of research: 23.11 million pounds of U3O8.
Storage: Canada and France.
Listed on AIM (London). Ticker: YCA. Trades in pence per share.

## URANIUM MARKET CONTEXT

Supply: Kazakhstan (Kazatomprom ~40%), Canada (Cameco), Namibia, Australia, Uzbekistan.
Demand: ~440 operating reactors globally. Inelastic — uranium is 5-10% of nuclear
plant operating cost. Utilities continue buying regardless of price.

Structural demand growth drivers (2026-2030):
  - China: 20+ new reactors under construction
  - India: significant capacity expansion
  - US/Europe: plant life extensions, SMR programmes
  - AI data centres: electricity demand driving nuclear power agreements
    (Microsoft/Constellation, Amazon/Talen Energy, Google/Kairos)
  - Japan: reactor restarts continuing after years of post-Fukushima shutdown

Supply concern: Kazatomprom has flagged production guidance cuts for 2025-2026 due to
sulfuric acid shortages and ground preparation delays. Supply side is not accelerating
to meet demand growth.

2026 spot price trajectory:
  Start of year: $80/lb
  Peak January 29: $101.41/lb (SPUT fund buying surge)
  Current June 12: $86.10/lb (flat, "cooldown in speculative rallies")
  TradeTech weekly: $86.25/lb (unchanged last week)

The spot market is currently in a waiting period. Utility buying remains primarily
through long-term contracts. Spot is thin but well-supported at $84-87/lb.
The Iran conflict created a commodity sentiment overhang that is now clearing.
