## THESIS OVERVIEW

The EU and UK are structurally forced to accelerate energy independence from LNG/O&G dependency. The Hormuz blockade has upgraded this from a policy trend to an existential urgency. The thesis rests on five converging structural drivers:

1. **Hormuz supply shock** — ~10-12M bpd locked out of global markets since Feb 2026. EU LNG import cost spikes = direct subsidy to EU domestic clean energy alternatives.
2. **Von der Leyen nuclear reversal** — EU taxonomy reclassification of nuclear as sustainable reopens €500B+ in green financing for nuclear projects.
3. **NATO rearmament / energy security convergence** — Defence budgets rising 3-5% GDP across EU. Energy security treated as national security. Electrification of military platforms = additional demand vector.
4. **EU Grids Package (Summer 2026)** — €584B in grid investment mandated by 2030. Creates a structural order backlog for grid cable and infrastructure names.
5. **Germany energy transition acceleration** — Post-coalition formation, Germany reversed nuclear stance, increased defence budget €108B. Rearmament + energy transition = dual demand driver for German-listed names.
