## WHAT MARA HAS BUILT — THREE TRANSFORMATIVE MOVES IN Q1 2026

### 1. STARWOOD JV (February 26, 2026)
Partner: Starwood Digital Ventures (Starwood Capital manages $125B+ assets)
Structure: MARA contributes powered sites. Starwood leads design, construction,
  tenant sourcing, financing, and operations. Capital-efficient for MARA.
Target capacity: ~1 GW near-term scaling beyond 2.5 GW over time
Markets targeted: Enterprise, hyperscale, AI customers
Stock reaction on announcement: +17%
Why it matters: Starwood brings the tenant relationships MARA does not have.
  This is not a speculative plan — it is a structured JV with a credible
  institutional operator with existing hyperscaler relationships and $125B AUM.
  Institutional credibility is the decisive factor for signing enterprise tenants.

### 2. EXAION ACQUISITION (February 20, 2026 — $174.5M for 64% stake)
Seller: EDF (French state-owned utility, one of the world's largest energy companies)
Assets received: Tier-III and Tier-IV data centres, 1,250+ enterprise-grade GPUs
Access gained: European sovereign cloud market — immediate revenue-generating HPC
Why it matters: Provides real HPC revenue now (not 2028), and gives MARA a
  European footprint with EDF as institutional anchor. Not speculative.

### 3. LONG RIDGE ENERGY & POWER (announced April 30, 2026 — ~$1.52B deal)
Seller: FTAI Infrastructure (FIP)
Assets: 505 MW combined-cycle gas turbine in Hannibal, Ohio
  + 1,600+ acres of land + water access + fiber + grid connections
Interconnection: PJM — one of the most active data centre and power markets
  in North America
Expected close: H2 2026
EBITDA contribution (power plant alone): $144M annualised
Capacity potential: 600+ gross MW of AI/HPC load, 1 GW+ total site potential
KEY DETAIL: Hannibal data centre (already co-located at this site) has inbound
  interest from MULTIPLE investment-grade AI/Critical IT tenants
Why it matters: MARA will own the power plant, the land, AND the data centre.
  Vertical integration from electron to compute. No other BTC miner has this
  at scale. Owning the generation asset removes the single largest bottleneck
  in AI data centre development — power access.

### ENERGY CAPACITY TRAJECTORY
  Start of 2024:      ~0.5 GW
  Q1 2026 operational: 1.9 GW (19 data centres, 4 continents)
  Post-Long Ridge (H2 2026): ~2.2 GW target
