## BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

MP Materials Corp is the only fully integrated rare earth operation in the United States.
Three distinct segments:

**1. Materials (Mountain Pass, California)**
Mining, crushing, separating, and refining rare earth elements from the only active
significant US rare earth mine. Produces NdPr oxide, the primary input for NdFeB
permanent magnets. Also produces heavy rare earths (terbium, dysprosium, gadolinium)
— the heavy rare earth separation circuit began commissioning Q2 2026.

**2. Magnetics (Independence facility, Fort Worth, Texas)**
Manufactures NdFeB permanent magnets on commercial equipment. Produced first
commercial NdFeB magnets December 2025. Current capacity ramping to 1,000 MT/year,
expanding to 3,000 MT/year via the Independence expansion anchored by Apple.

**3. 10X Facility (Northlake, Texas — under construction)**
New greenfield magnet facility targeting 7,000 MT/year capacity. Ground broken 2026.
Commissioning target: 2028. $1B JPMorgan/Goldman financing committed. DoD is anchor
customer with 100% offtake commitment for first 10 years of production.

## RISKS — STATED DIRECTLY

**1. Valuation is entirely dependent on 2028 commissioning**
If 10X is delayed by 12-18 months — a real risk for greenfield industrial construction
— the DCF shifts materially. A 2029 commissioning instead of 2028 reduces NPV by ~15%.
This is a material risk.

**2. Trump administration policy reversal**
The DoD deal was structured under Trump. Change of administration or policy direction
could alter the deal structure. The agreements are contractual and would require DoD
to breach for cancellation — unlikely but not zero. This is the single biggest
tail risk.

**3. China NdPr price manipulation**
China controls 60%+ of global processing. If China dumps NdPr below $110/kg for
sustained periods, the DoD floor becomes the only thing protecting MP's revenue.
The floor is contractual and real — but NdPr below $110 means zero upside above floor
for years.

**4. Current earnings carry no weight**
FY2026 EPS of $0.33 on a $3.6B market cap means the current P/E is ~209x.
If patience runs out in the market before 2028 commissioning, the stock can de-rate
significantly purely on sentiment, even with the thesis intact.

**5. Construction execution**
10X is a $1.25B construction project for a company that has never built a factory
at this scale. Cost overruns, permitting delays, and labour shortages are real.
