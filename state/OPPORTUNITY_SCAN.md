## VERDICT DEFINITIONS

CONFIRMED: primary source independently supports the claim. Actioned or logged elsewhere,
  state where (DECISION_REGISTER.md row, intelligence\ file, or journal entry).
REJECTED: primary source contradicts the claim, or the claim does not hold up under a
  direct check. No further action, but the row stays as the record of the check.
NEEDS MORE WORK: primary source check inconclusive, or not completed this session. Carries
  forward with a stated next step. Does not silently disappear, gets picked up next session
  open until it resolves to CONFIRMED or REJECTED.
