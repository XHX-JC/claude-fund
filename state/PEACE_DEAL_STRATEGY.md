## THE PEACE DEAL PORTFOLIO — MINIMUM 4:1 R/R REQUIRED

### Guiding Principles
- **Only enter peace deal longs where R/R is 4:1 or higher** — this screens out airlines near ATH (DAL, UAL already re-rated) and focuses on names that have been maximally squeezed by oil
- **Distressed beats quality** in this specific trade — the bigger the oil pain, the bigger the re-rating
- **Unhedged fuel exposure = maximum re-rating** — CCL (zero hedges) > RCL (60% hedged) > NCLH (51% hedged) in terms of earnings recovery magnitude per $1 oil decline
- **Downside is naturally limited** — these stocks have already been beaten down. At cheap valuations with strong underlying demand, the floor is not far below current prices
- **The thesis is mechanical, not speculative** — when oil drops from $97 to $65 on a deal, cruise line earnings recover by a specific and calculable amount. This is not a sentiment trade

### The Cruise Liner Core Thesis
Cruise lines are the optimal vehicle because:
1. **Fuel is 12-15%+ of operating costs** — unhedged direct P&L impact of oil price
2. **Demand is NOT the problem** — bookings at record levels, deposits at $8B+, 85% capacity booked at high prices. Oil is the ONLY headwind. Remove it and earnings recover mechanically.
3. **Mideast route disruption** — TUI ships repositioned out of region, Med bookings softened. Peace = immediate route restoration and booking surge
4. **Consumer confidence** — high fuel prices have suppressed discretionary travel sentiment. Peace = consumer confidence rebound
