## FOUR CYCLE PHASES
| Phase | What it looks like |
|-------|--------------------|
| Bottom Not Reached | Support breaks and fails to reclaim. ETF outflows persist. Leverage cascades continue. On-chain cost basis lost. Macro risk-off. |
| Bottom Forming | Panic selling slows. BTC defends a support band. Liquidations cool. Fear stabilises. ETF outflows shrink. Whales/LTHs accumulate. |
| Trend Repair | BTC reclaims a key resistance band. STH realised price reclaimed. SOPR improves. ETF flows stabilise or turn positive. |
| Buyers Piling Back In | BTC holds above reclaim levels. ETF inflows persist. Spot volume rises. ETH/BTC and alt breadth improve. Leverage rebuilds gradually. |

## UNIVERSAL SUPPORT BAND TEST
Run whenever BTC reaches or breaks any support band.

| Test | Question | Constructive answer | Bearish answer |
|------|----------|---------------------|----------------|
| 1. Price reaction | Wick below and reclaim, or decisive close below? | Wick and reclaim; higher low forms | Daily/weekly close below; failed reclaim |
| 2. Volume | Sell volume absorbed or accelerating? | High volume without further downside; green volume rises | High volume breakdown; weak bounce volume |
| 3. ETF flows | Outflows slowing or reversing? | Outflows shrink; neutral/positive days appear | Persistent large outflows; streak extends |
| 4. Derivatives | Leverage flushed? | OI down sharply, liquidations cool, funding neutral/negative | OI elevated, fresh liquidation cascades |
| 5. Sentiment | Fear extreme but stabilising? | Fear & Greed below 20 then improves | Fear remains extreme and price keeps falling |
| 6. On-chain cost basis | Price holding/reclaiming realised-price levels? | Reclaim STH realised price; realised price holds | Price remains below cost basis; retests fail |
| 7. Profit/loss (SOPR) | Realised losses peaking? | SOPR recovers towards/above 1 | SOPR below 1 and falling |
| 8. Whales/LTHs | Strong hands accumulating? | Exchange outflows rise; whales add | Exchange inflows rise; whales distribute |
| 9. Breadth | ETH/BTC and alts stabilising? | ETH/BTC stops falling after BTC stabilises | Alt breadth keeps deteriorating |
| 10. Macro | Risk liquidity stabilising? | DXY/yields stable or lower; risk assets stop falling | Dollar/yields rise; equities weaken |

## KNOWN LIMITATIONS OF THIS FRAMEWORK
1. ETF flows and macro signals are increasingly correlated — both reflect institutional
   risk appetite. They provide less independent confirmation than the scorecard implies.
   Treat them as partial confirmation of the same signal, not two separate signals.

2. On-chain whale data is partially blind to ETF-based exposure changes.
   A whale reducing via IBIT redemptions does not appear as an exchange inflow.
   Weight on-chain whale signals at ~70% of face value in the current ETF era.

3. SOPR, MVRV, NUPL require Glassnode for real-time data. Free alternatives (Checkonchain,
   Bitcoin Magazine Pro) are 1-3 days delayed. Use as directional signals, not precise timing.

4. The scorecard requires 6 of 9 for a bottom signal. In fast-moving markets, waiting
   for 6/9 may mean entering after a 15-20% bounce from the absolute low. This is
   acceptable — the goal is confirmation, not catching the exact bottom.
