## Strategy & Positioning

- **Specialty and premium only.** Savannah Fields does not offer commodity coffee. No Robusta bulk, no below-specialty grades.
- **Cup scores 80–90+** (SCA standard). Minimum 80 to be offered. Typical range 83–88, exceptional lots above 90.
- **Not competing with the majors** (Kyagalanyi/Volcafe, Olam, ETG, Louis Dreyfus). USP is direct grower access, traceability, EUDR compliance, and quality guarantee — not volume or price.
- **Origins:** Mount Elgon (Bugisu region, eastern Uganda) and Rwenzori Highlands (western Uganda). Both high-altitude Arabica.
- **Processing:** Washed, natural dried, honey — buyer preference accommodated at order stage.

## Commercial Model

### Pricing
- **Cost:** approximately $7/kg from growers
- **Sale price:** $12–$15/kg FOB Mombasa
- **Gross margin:** approximately 50%
- Priced above MAAIF daily reference — premium justified by cup score, traceability, EUDR documentation, quality guarantee

### Volumes
- **Minimum for sea freight:** 5,000 kg
- **Below 5,000 kg:** air freight (higher cost, faster delivery)
- No standard container-load minimum — unlike commodity exporters

### Payment Terms (CONFIRMED)
- **50% deposit** on contract confirmation
- **50% on sight of Bill of Lading** (sea freight) or **airfreight confirmation** (air shipments)
- **No Letter of Credit** — LC currently not working well with Ugandan banks. Not to be offered or mentioned to clients.

### Lead Time
- Orders placed with growers **1 month in advance**
- Crop ready approximately **4–6 weeks** after order
- Export-ready: **approximately 8 weeks** from order placement
- Full timeline order to European destination: **10–12 weeks**

---

## Quality & Compliance

- **UCDA certification** on every shipment (Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, Quality Certificate)
- **SCA cup score report** from allocated crop issued to buyer before shipment — no physical pre-shipment samples sent (adds 1 week delay)
- **EUDR compliant** — farm-plot level traceability built in from day one
- **Written quality guarantee** — replacement or refund if lot does not match agreed cup profile
- No physical samples sent to buyers — cup score report from actual allocated crop is the buyer assurance

---

## Key Sales Messages

1. Direct access to growers — not available through commodity trading houses
2. Every lot has a name, region, cooperative, and geolocation
3. EUDR documentation is standard, not an add-on
4. Cup score from the actual allocated crop, not a representative sample
5. Fixed pricing at contract execution — no daily market movement after order placed
6. Uganda specialty is undervalued vs Ethiopia/Kenya — window to lock in relationships now
