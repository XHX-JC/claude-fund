## Framing Decisions — Locked

### Sovereign / Geopolitical Language
- **Do not state:** "structurally free of US and Chinese control", "independent of US/Chinese vendors"
- **Do state:** "data sovereignty", "data under your control", "answers to no jurisdiction but your own", "sovereign-native architecture"
- **US and China:** Named as established market leaders — the reference point, not the adversary
- **The investor infers** independence and geopolitical security from the sovereignty framing. Never stated explicitly.
- **Locai's position:** "The sovereign alternative" — not anti-US/China, simply a different category

### Investor Audience
Primary: Corporate, sovereign entity, institutional, state-level organisations  
Secondary: Family offices and private investors with UK/EU/Gulf exposure  
Tone: Goldman Sachs / Rothschild advisory standard — authoritative, evidence-first, no startup hyperbole
