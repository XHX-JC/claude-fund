## Technology — Forget-Me-Not™

- Core post-training framework — prevents catastrophic forgetting when fine-tuning on new domain data  
- Patent filed: EP25211856 (filed 28 Oct 2025)  
- Self-improvement loop — generates and refines own training data (AlphaGo Zero-inspired)  
- Enables debiasing, decensoring, UK alignment of open-source base models  
- **Critical nuance:** This is post-training engineering on open-source bases (Qwen, Nemotron). Valuable commercially, but NOT independent pretraining. "Foundational AI company" framing in the deck overstates this.

## Existing Deck Assessment — Honest Critique

**What works:**
- Sovereign AI positioning is timely and real
- Project Mercury / Civo partnership is credible
- First Light Fusion case study is the strongest concrete proof point
- Team slide (Lord Drayson especially) carries weight

**What fails:**
- Gradient-heavy aesthetic is generic startup, not institutional
- "British Foundational AI" headline is misleading — they post-train, not pretrain (yet)
- Benchmark claims are self-reported, not independently validated
- Financial slide has no actual revenue numbers
- Sovereign Landscape comparison table places Locai at £1.25M vs competitors at $500M+ — makes the gap look vast, not the ambition look bold
- No clear articulation of *why Locai wins* in concrete commercial terms
- No risk/mitigation section — institutional investors will expect this
- Privacy claims contradicted by their own privacy policy (GCP sub-processor, human review)
